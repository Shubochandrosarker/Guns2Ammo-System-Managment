Check-In Cheat Sheet

A member walks in

  1. Ask them to open their member card and show the QR code (in their account → Digital Member Card).
  2. Scan it. The screen shows their photo, plan, and two badges: ✓ Active ✓ Waiver on file
  3. If you see ⚠ Waiver not signed or ⚠ expired, have them sign at the kiosk before shooting.

A guest / first-timer walks in

  1. Point them to the kiosk tablet or the QR poster at the counter.
  2. They tap "Guest / First Visit — Sign Waiver," read it, type their name + email, and submit.
  3. The screen returns to the start for the next person. They're cleared to shoot.

"My payment didn't go through / I need to update my card"

  1. Send them to /account/ → Update Payment Method (they manage it themselves through Stripe).
  2. Or, from the admin, send them a Stripe payment link.

Recording a paper waiver someone signed in store

  1. Go to Memberistic → Waivers, find the member, click Docs.
  2. Click Mark waiver signed, and optionally Upload a photo/scan of the paper.

Where things live (admin)

  1. Kiosk page: /check-in/ · Guest waiver: Waivers → Open guest waiver page
  2. Print the QR poster: Waivers → Print check-in QR poster
  3. Export all signed waivers: Waivers → Download all signed waivers (CSV)
  4. Members, payments, check-ins, activity: the Memberistic menu
Keep the kiosk tablet locked to the /check-in/ page. Questions → your web team. Prepared by WordPressistic.
