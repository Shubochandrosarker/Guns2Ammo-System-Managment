Membership & Check-In System

How it works, and everything your team and members can use today.

How it works

1. Joining. A customer picks a plan and checks out through Stripe. Their account, digital membership card, and record are created automatically, and they receive a welcome email with links to their account, waiver, and lane booking.
2. Automatic billing. Monthly plans charge on the same day every month, annual plans on the same day every year — anchored to the day they joined. Renewal dates are set automatically.
3. Reminders & receipts. Members get a reminder about a week before renewal and again the day before. After each charge they receive a payment receipt and a renewal confirmation. Failed payments trigger an "update your card" notice.
4. Self-service. From their account page members update their card, view billing history, switch plans, or cancel — no call to the desk.
5. Waivers. Anyone can sign the range waiver from any device in under a minute — no login. Each signature stores the date, signer, and exact wording agreed to, and expires after a year. Guests and walk-ins can sign too.
6. At the door. Staff scan the member's QR code and instantly see if the membership is active and whether the waiver is on file or expired. Guests scan a poster or use the tablet to sign before shooting.

What members can use

Member account /account/

  • Digital membership card with personal QR
  • Update payment method / fix a failed charge
  • Billing history with payment references
  • Switch monthly ↔ annual, or cancel
  • Plan, renewal date, linked/family members
  • Booking history
  • Sign the waiver & see its status
  • Upload documents (waiver, ID) — private

Automatic emails

  • Welcome / membership activated
  • Payment receipt (every charge)
  • Renewal confirmation
  • Renewal reminders (~7 days & ~24 hours out)
  • Payment failed — update card
  • Membership expired
  • Waiver needed (one-click sign link)

What your team can use

Waivers Memberistic → Waivers

  • Self-serve & guest/non-member signing
  • Counts: signed / missing / expired
  • Mark paper waivers signed (one or bulk)
  • Email the waiver link to everyone missing
  • Edit waiver wording & validity period
  • Download full signed-waiver archive (CSV)
  • Print any single waiver as PDF
  • Upload/download member documents

Check-in

  • Kiosk page /check-in/ for a counter tablet
  • Printable QR poster — customers scan to sign & check in
  • Staff QR scan shows live membership + waiver status

Behind the scenes

  • Member directory, plans, payments, check-ins, activity
  • Tools to set renewal dates & connect imported members to Stripe
  • Reporting on members, renewals, waivers

To switch everything on (one-time, handled by your web team)

1. Stripe — enable the customer billing portal and the payment webhook (powers auto-charging, receipts, and the "update card" button).
2. Email delivery — set up the domain's email authentication so member emails reach the inbox, not spam.
3. Existing members — move legacy/imported members onto a Stripe subscription so they auto-renew (self-serve from their account, or a staff payment link).
