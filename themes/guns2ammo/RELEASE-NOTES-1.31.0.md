# WPistic Theme 1.31.0

Release date: 2026-08-30

## Highlights

- Completes the Ladies Tuesday event data with a concrete upcoming occurrence, full venue and organizer details, image, offer validity date, description, and end date.
- Aligns canonical URLs, robots directives, XML sitemaps, AI-readable indexes, and private/transactional page exclusions around one shared policy.
- Removes duplicate Organization, Product, Breadcrumb, and homepage FAQ structured data sources.
- Grounds Product structured data in real catalog values and avoids synthetic price, MPN, shipping, and availability claims.
- Improves article author identity and protects schema/sitemap dates from imported or scheduled date inversions.
- Disables public Rank Math score badges and credit links at the theme layer.
- Adds conservative browser security headers without guessing a checkout-breaking Content Security Policy.
- Retires the obsolete Free CCW promotion URL with a one-hop redirect and updates internal navigation to the current certification page.

## Deployment notes

- Deploy the theme and the four versioned must-use plugins together.
- Purge page, object, CDN, and sitemap caches after activation.
- Revalidate Ladies Tuesday, a product, an article, the homepage, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, and llms-full.txt.
- Course duration, live-fire, reciprocity, and pricing claims on CCW content still require the business owner to select one authoritative current offer before editorial consolidation.
